# In-vivo plots

Plots body weight, tumor volume and survival from a mouse study workbook (`.xlsx`) or an older study note (Markdown) chosen on your device. The file is read in the browser and never uploaded.

## Use

1. Open the app and tap **Choose file**, then pick the study's `.xlsx` workbook (or `.md` note) from the vault folder.
2. The app draws a dashboard. On a desktop screen it fills the window: the weight row and the tumor row each show the mean per group or cage, one line per mouse, and one bar per mouse at the latest session; below them are the survival curve, the latest-values table and a heat map of every measurement, then (with a schedule) what is coming up, and the injection calendar and table. On a narrower desktop window (under 1500 px) each row's bar chart runs full width under its two line charts, so weight comes first and tumor below it. On a phone the same views stack in one column. A bar under the toolbar (**Weight · Tumor · Survival · Latest values · All measurements · Coming up · Injections**) stays at the top of the screen as you scroll: tap a topic to jump to it; the topic in view is highlighted.
   - body weight as % change (lines at −15% and −20%) or grams
   - tumor volume (longer × shorter² / 2) or relative to each mouse's first measured volume, on a linear or log axis (volume charts shade 50–100; **Shaded 50–100: Hide** turns it off)
   - injections (from the workbook's Injections sheet) as a strip of ▲ under the weight, tumor and survival charts, one row per group or cage; hover or tap it to see the drug, dose and volume and how many mice got it. A hollow ▲ means only some mice in that group or cage were dosed. An **Injection calendar** shows the same doses as month grids (one chip per group or cage and drug, with how many of its mice were dosed; dashed means only some; ● marks measurement days), and an Injections table lists every mouse's drug on each injection day. Mice that have not started treatment yet are not counted as missing a dose
   - on the individual lines, ◆ marks where a mouse moved cage (or got a new tail mark) and ✕ where it was euthanized, found dead or removed; the tooltip gives the cage and tail mark on that day
   - survival (Kaplan–Meier) per group or cage
   - a latest-values table with each mouse's current cage and tail mark, flagging weight loss ≥ 15% / ≥ 20%, mice that have ended (with date, study day and reason) and mice with no data in the latest session; click a column to sort
3. The toolbar above the charts sets what every view shows: **Group by** (the workbook's groups, each mouse's current cage, or your own groups), which groups or cages (click a chip to hide or show one, double-click to show only that one), weight and tumor units, error bars (SEM, SD or none), x axis as date or study day, a highlighted mouse (also set by clicking a line, bar or table row), and the theme. The settings are remembered on the device. On a phone only Group by and the group or cage chips show; tap **More settings** for the rest.
   - **Group** uses the Group column of the workbook's Mice sheet. A group can span cages and a cage can hold several groups.
   - **Cage** puts each mouse under the cage it is in now, with its whole history, so a cage's mean is the mean of its current mice.
   - **Treatment start** (when mice have one: their own `Treatment start` on the Mice sheet, for staggered enrollment, or else their group's on the Groups sheet): **X axis: From start** lines the mice up so day 0 is each mouse's own start (negative days are before it; doses and survival follow). Two toggles then appear: **Before day 0** hides the days before the start, and **After a group ends** cuts the charts at the last day of the shown group whose data ends first; slide images follow them. **Change from: Treatment start** measures weight % change and relative tumor volume from each mouse's last value on or before its start instead of its first measurement. The −15% / −20% lines are then left off and the weight-loss flags stay on the first weight. Means and each mouse's own line are both plotted on exact days from start. Mice with no start are left out of these views (the chart text says how many), and the latest-values table gains **Start** and **Days treated** columns.
   - **Schedule** (when the Regimens sheet has `Days`, or the Groups sheet a measurement cadence): **Coming up** lists the next 7 days: measurement days, and each dose due from each mouse's start, with the mice's cages and tail marks. Below it are doses planned since the latest data that are not entered yet, **missed** doses (planned on or before the latest session or injection, with no Injections row) and doses **off schedule** (a scheduled drug on a day not planned, or before the mouse has a start). The injection calendar adds planned doses (dotted), missed ones (red) and the coming measurement days (○), and a tile at the top counts missed doses.
4. **Make groups** opens a panel for planning groups on this device: tick mice (all, those with a tumor in the latest session, those with tumor volume ≥ a value, or one by one; mice that have ended are skipped), add and name groups, then put the ticked mice in a group, or spread them evenly across all groups balanced by tumor volume or body weight (click again to re-roll), or at random. Each group's n and mean ± SEM tumor volume, weight and weight change at the latest session are shown so you can check the groups are balanced. **Group by: My groups** draws every view by these groups. They are kept on this device for this file (by file name); **Copy for workbook** copies Mouse ID and group in the Mice sheet's order to put them in the workbook, and **Copy table**, **Copy for note** (Markdown) and **CSV** take them out too.
5. Tools: each chart has a **slide** button that makes an image for a 16:9 PowerPoint slide, whatever your screen size: under a slide title, full slide, half (two side by side), or this chart with its weight or tumor counterpart. Text is about 15 pt on the slide, the background white, dark or none, and the chart title on the image or not; a preview shows it on a slide, then copy or download it. The downloaded PNG inserts (Insert → Pictures) at the size shown; a pasted copy keeps its shape but may need resizing. The **copy** button copies a slide image with the last settings in one click. Each chart also downloads as CSV and opens full screen (Esc closes it). The tables copy as tab-separated text for Excel or Prism. The toolbar's **Export** menu makes a weight + tumor slide, saves all the charts as one PNG, every measurement and derived value as one CSV (with the cage and tail mark on each date), or prints (save as PDF from the print dialog).
6. The file's data stays in this browser's storage, so the plots are there next time. Choose the file again after new data, or tap **Forget file**.

**Load demo** shows synthetic data (`demo/synthetic-demo.xlsx`). After the first visit the app works offline and can be installed to the home screen.

Rows the app can't read are listed under the header, with the sheet and row (workbook) or line number (note).

## Workbook format

- **Measurements**: `Date`, `Cage`, `Tail mark`, `Mouse ID`, `Weight (g)`, `Tumor Length`, `Tumor Breadth`, `Notes`, one row per mouse per session. Cage and tail mark are where the mouse was that day.
- **Mice**: `Mouse ID`, `Sex`, `Group`, `Status` (`alive`, `euthanized`, `found dead`, `removed`), `End date`, `End reason`, `Notes`, and optionally `Treatment start` (the mouse's own start, e.g. the day it was enrolled; blank means its group's), one row per mouse.
- **Injections** (optional): `Mouse ID`, `Date`, `Drug`, `Dose`, `Volume (µL)`, `Route`, `Notes`, one row per mouse per dose. Only Mouse ID, Date and Drug are required; other columns (such as `Group` or `Cage ID`) are ignored.
- **Groups** (optional): `Group`, `Treatment start`, one row per group, the group named as on the Mice sheet (case ignored). Only these two columns are read, and only when both are there; other columns (`Color`, `Mice`, `Drugs`, helper columns) are ignored. A group with no start, none of whose mice has a start of its own, is reported under the header. Two labelled cells anywhere on the sheet set the measurement cadence: `Measure every (days)` and `Measure from` (a measurement date), each with its value in the next cell to the right.
- **Regimens** (optional): `Group`, `Drug`, `Dose`, `Volume (µL)`, `Route`, `Days`, one line per drug a group gets. `Days` are whole days from each mouse's treatment start to give it, separated by commas (`0` or `0, 2, 4`); empty means the drug is not on a schedule. Read only when it has Group, Drug and Days. Keep the Days column formatted as Text in Excel, or a list like `10,100` turns into a number.
- **Locations** (optional, used by Excel, not read by the app): `Animal ID`, then one column per date mice were placed or moved, each cell `Cage-Tail mark` (e.g. `20184-2`), meaning where the mouse is from that date on. Fill it in by hand on move days (copy the last column, date it, change the mice that moved). The Measurements `Mouse ID` column is then a formula that finds the mouse from date, cage and tail mark, so in the lab you only write down cage and tail mark; a red, empty ID means nobody is at that place on Locations.

Columns are matched by name, in any order. `Animal ID` is read as `Mouse ID` and `Cage ID` as `Cage`; other columns (such as `Current cage` or `Current tail mark` on the Mice sheet) are ignored. An empty cell means "not measured". See `PLAN.md` → *Input format*; the same contract is documented in the Obsidian vault (`02 Notes/In-vivo Plots.md`). The older note format (one `## YYYY-MM-DD` heading and table per session) is still read.

## Develop

No build step. Serve the folder and open it:

```sh
python3 -m http.server   # http://localhost:8000
node --test test.js      # parsers and derived values (synthetic data only)
node --test private/private.test.js   # the real study, if you have it
uv run --no-project --with playwright==1.64.1 python tools/smoke_test.py [note.md]   # headless-browser check; set CHROMIUM=/path/to/chrome to reuse a browser
```

On Windows without Node, VS Code's own Node works: `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE=1 "<VS Code>/Code.exe" --test test.js`.

- `vendor/chart.umd.min.js` is Chart.js 4.4.1 and `vendor/xlsx.mini.min.js` is SheetJS 0.20.3 (from cdn.sheetjs.com), vendored for offline use. SheetJS is loaded only when a workbook is opened.
- `sw.js` caches the app shell; bump `CACHE` when the file list changes.
- `.nojekyll` makes GitHub Pages serve files as-is (otherwise Jekyll turns `demo/*.md` into HTML).
- Workbooks: `node tools/note-to-workbook.js note.md > rows.json` (or `--demo`), then `powershell -File tools/build-workbook.ps1 -Json rows.json -Out "C:\full\path.xlsx"` builds the formatted workbook with Excel (Windows only): tables, dropdowns for Status, End reason and Mouse ID, frozen header row.
- Schedule: `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/add-schedule.ps1 -Path "C:\full\path\Study Data.xlsx"` adds to a study workbook (Excel 365, Windows; it saves a backup copy first and can be run again): Mice `Treatment start` and `Start used`, Regimens `Days`, the measurement cadence on Groups (K1:L2), a Checklist that shows only the drugs due on its date (B1, today by default) and greys the measurements on days without them, a Plan sheet (alive mice × the next 21 days), and on Injected Drugs marks "missed" only for mice that had started by that day.
- Icons: `uv run --no-project --with pillow tools/make-icons.py`.
- Real study files go in `private/` (gitignored), never in the repo.
